The problem

The answer is somewhere in thousands of documents.

On an EPC project, the answer to an engineering question is spread across specifications, standards, regulations, datasheets and vendor documents and their revisions, often in more than one language. Engineers lose hours finding the right clause, and a missed requirement costs money and safety.

What DocAgent does

An agent, not just a chatbot.

A chatbot answers one question. An agent plans several steps and uses tools: it searches the documents, reads the right clauses, compares them and prepares a result.

Answers with proof

Every statement cites the document, clause and page. Hover a number to read the passage; click to see it outlined in the original.

"Not found" instead of guessing

When the documents don't cover the question, DocAgent says so, and shows the closest passages it checked.

Works across languages

Ask in Korean and get the answer from an English standard, or the other way round.

Works with your existing system

Designed to connect to your CDE or document management system. You don't have to replace it.

A person stays in control

The agent prepares comments, checks and status suggestions, and a person approves them. Approvals are formal project records.

Measured, not assumed

Search methods compared on a bilingual test set; answers graded for faithfulness to their sources.
